# EGRISS identification questions and the STATAFRIC modules

A note for discussion with STATAFRIC STG 8 (African Union): where the revised EGRISS identification questions for refugees and IDPs stand, and how they compare with the STATAFRIC refugee and IDP modules (Draft 0).

Live page: https://mitrovif.github.io/statafric-egriss-idq/

## What is in it

1. Context: why the 2023 EGRISS questions are being revised, and the timeline to the second edition (2027)
2. Desk review: map of 32 questionnaires, what they capture and miss, how surveys ask the hardest questions, and each questionnaire's route
3. Testing with respondents: Thailand, Nigeria (NBS) and remote testing
4. The revised EGRISS questions, in full, with a walk-through you can answer
5. Side by side: the STATAFRIC modules and the revised module, and who ends up where
6. Every question, word for word
7. What the STATAFRIC draft already does well
8. Points for discussion

## Editing

Everything is in one file, `index.html` (styles and scripts included). Edit it here on GitHub or locally; every push to `main` updates the live page within a minute or two.

Ratings and wordings come from the EGRISS identification questions task team materials (desk review, question bank, cognitive testing packs). Draft for discussion, not for citation.
